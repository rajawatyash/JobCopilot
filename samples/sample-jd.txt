About the role
We are looking for a Lead .NET Engineer to lead a team of 5 engineers building our
event-driven trading platform.

What you'll do
- Design and build microservices in C# and .NET 8 running on Kubernetes (AKS).
- Own our event streaming with Apache Kafka.
- Work with PostgreSQL and Redis for data and caching.
- Set technical direction, run design reviews and mentor engineers.
- Improve our CI/CD pipelines and observability (OpenTelemetry, Grafana).

What we're looking for
- 8+ years of commercial C# / .NET experience.
- Strong experience with microservices and event-driven architecture.
- Hands-on Kubernetes and Docker.
- Kafka or a similar message broker.
- Experience leading or mentoring a team.
- Nice to have: React, Terraform, financial services background.
